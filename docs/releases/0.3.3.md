# V0.3.3 public preview / 公开预览

This release adds quota recovery history that is independent of task control. Successful, fresh service observations can produce an Activity record even when there are no managed tasks, automatic control is paused, or notifications are disabled. Both native interfaces show the observations and the basis for inferred attribution.

本次增加独立于任务控制的额度恢复记录。取得有效、及时的服务采样后，即使没有受管任务、自动控制已暂停或通知已关闭，也可生成操作记录。Mac 与 Windows 原生界面均展示前后采样数据和来源推断依据。

## Changes / 更新

- **Independent observation:** restoration is recorded from comparable quota samples rather than being tied to a task's resume operation. / **独立观察：**根据可比较的额度采样记录恢复，与任务是否执行续接分开处理。
- **Four source labels:** inferred user-initiated reset, scheduled service reset, independent service reset, or unattributed recovery. Details retain each affected window's remaining percentage, reset deadline, credit counts, and observation times. / **四类来源：**推断的用户主动恢复、官方正常周期重置、官方独立重置或未归因恢复，详情保留各受影响窗口的余额、刷新时间、权益数量及采样时间。
- **Restart-safe history:** account-and-quota-bucket baselines and events are persisted in one transaction. Repeated or older samples do not duplicate an observed change. / **重启防重：**按账号与额度类别，在同一事务中保存基线和事件；重复或更早采样不会重复写入已观察变化。
- **Baseline on first use:** the first valid observation establishes a starting point. Earlier activity that day and changes across an account or bucket switch are not reconstructed. / **首次建立基线：**首次有效观察先确定起点，不补写当天较早历史，也不跨账号或额度类别切换推算恢复。
- **Delayed credit changes:** a 120-second attribution observation period follows changes in credit counts, inventory, support status, or fields, including samples with no balance increase. This prevents a later stable inventory from prematurely implying an official reset. / **权益延迟保护：**权益数量、清单、支持状态或字段变化后保留 120 秒来源观察期，即使当次余额没有回升也生效，避免将稍后的稳定清单过早解释为官方重置。
- **New-cycle visibility:** a credible full-period rollover is recorded even when new spending masks any net balance gain; details explicitly mark that the net balance did not increase. / **新周期可见：**可信的完整周期推进即使被新增消耗抵消，也会记录，并明确标注净余额未上涨。
- **Native activity previews:** the release gallery highlights quota recovery records on both platforms, alongside main windows, settings, and the menu panel. All screenshots use synthetic data. / **原生记录预览：**发行图库重点展示两平台额度恢复记录，同时包含主窗、设置和菜单面板；全部截图使用示例数据。

## Reading the source labels / 如何理解来源

Attribution is inferred from changes between observations. The service does not provide a direct reset-cause receipt for this feature, and `official_reason` remains null. A source label is not confirmation that a person clicked a reset button or that OpenAI ran a particular promotion.

来源由前后采样变化推断。此功能未获得服务直接提供的重置原因回执，`official_reason` 保持空值；来源标签不能确认有人点击了重置，也不能确认某次 OpenAI 活动。

- **User-initiated reset:** requires balance restoration correlated with reconcilable credit-inventory and count evidence. A count decrease alone is insufficient. / **用户主动恢复：**余额恢复同时出现能够核对的权益清单与数量变化，仅凭可用次数减少不足以判断。
- **Scheduled service reset:** requires a credible rollover near the previous deadline, with comparable credit inventory remaining stable. / **官方正常周期重置：**原截止时间附近发生可信的周期推进，且可比权益清单保持稳定。
- **Independent service reset:** requires a substantial balance increase before the previous deadline, with comparable credit inventory remaining stable. / **官方独立重置：**原截止时间前发生显著余额回升，且可比权益清单保持稳定。
- **Unattributed recovery:** retained when evidence is missing, inconsistent, incomplete, or too far apart in time. Credit expiry and truncated lists are not assumed to mean credit use. Differing classifications across windows produce an unattributed overall record with per-window details. / **未归因恢复：**证据缺失、不一致、不完整或采样相隔过久时保留未归因；不将权益过期、清单截断假定为权益使用。多个窗口结论不同时，整体记录未归因，详情保留逐窗口结果。

### Detection boundaries / 检测边界

A balance rise of at least **5 percentage points** is recorded even when the reset deadline stays unchanged. A smaller positive rise can be recorded when the deadline advances credibly, with insufficient evidence remaining unattributed. A cycle transition is also recorded when the samples cross the previous deadline and the reset deadline advances by approximately one complete window. For example, a new cycle with a net balance of 98% → 97% is retained as a cycle observation and marked as having no net balance increase.

余额回升至少 **5 个百分点**时，即使刷新截止时间不变也会记录；较小的正向回升伴随可信的截止时间推进时也可记录，依据不足时保留未归因。采样跨越原截止时间、刷新时间推进约一个完整窗口时，同样记录周期变化；例如新周期净余额为 98% → 97%，仍保留周期观察，并标明净余额未上涨。

Deadline jitter tolerance is 2% of the window duration, bounded to **5–60 seconds**. The band immediately before the old deadline is insufficient to distinguish a scheduled reset from an independent early reset. An observation gap longer than the lesser of **6 hours** and **one window plus its tolerance** prevents confident source attribution. Changed or ambiguous window identities are also treated conservatively. These are history-detection boundaries; editable task pause and recovery rules remain separate.

截止时间抖动容差为窗口长度的 2%，限制在 **5–60 秒**。原截止时间前的容差区间不足以区分正常周期与提前独立重置。采样间隔超过 **6 小时**与**一个窗口加容差**两者中的较小值时，不进行确定性的来源归因；窗口身份发生变化或存在歧义时同样保守处理。这些属于历史检测边界，与可编辑的任务暂停／恢复规则分开生效。

During the **120-second** credit-change observation period, a later balance increase with otherwise stable credits remains unattributed. A credit disappearing from a complete available inventory with a matching count decrease in the same sample can support an inferred user reset when its expiry is absent by explicit null or is more than 60 seconds after the observation; an explicit reconciled consumed status can also provide evidence. Neither case supplies a direct official action receipt, and a count drop alone is insufficient. Records are written immediately and are not retrospectively reclassified when later credit changes arrive.

在 **120 秒**权益变化观察期内，随后出现的余额回升即使对应权益已经稳定，也保留未归因。同一采样中，完整清单内的可用权益消失且数量同步减少，并且权益明确无到期时间或距当前采样到期超过 60 秒，可支持推断的用户主动恢复；能够核对的明确已使用状态也可作为依据。两者均不提供官方直接操作回执，仅凭次数减少不足以判断。记录即时写入，稍后出现的权益变化不会追溯改写此前归因。

The application must be running and able to collect service data. Offline changes cannot be fully reconstructed. Reset credits remain read-only and are never redeemed automatically. Existing pause/resume thresholds, consecutive confirmation, account checks, per-chat authorization, manual-intervention checks, and uncertain-delivery safeguards remain in effect. An Activity record itself does not authorize a task or send a message.

应用需要运行并能够采集服务数据，无法完整重建离线期间的变化。重置权益保持只读，不自动兑换。既有暂停／恢复阈值、连续确认、账号检查、聊天授权、人工接管检查和发送结果不确定时的保护继续适用；操作记录本身不授予任务许可，也不发送指令。

## Packages and upgrade / 安装包与升级

Choose a V0.3.3 preview from [Releases](https://github.com/Dddysun/Codex-Mission-Manager/releases). Each immutable `v0.3.3-preview.N` tag identifies its source commit and package set:

- `Codex-Mission-Manager-0.3.3-arm64-preview.dmg` — Apple Silicon, macOS 14+.
- `Codex-Mission-Manager-0.3.3-windows-x64-setup.exe` — current-user installation, Windows 10 build 19041+, x64, .NET Framework 4.8+.
- `Codex-Mission-Manager-0.3.3-windows-x64-preview.zip` — portable Windows package.

Both platforms bundle the backend and a pinned Python runtime. Quit the previous version before upgrading. Configuration, task permissions, rules, schedules, and existing records remain in the separate application-data directory. The first valid quota observation after this upgrade starts the new history baseline. See the [user guide](https://github.com/Dddysun/Codex-Mission-Manager/blob/main/docs/USER_GUIDE.md).

两平台均附带后端及固定 Python 环境。升级前退出旧版；配置、任务权限、运行规则、预约和原有记录保存在独立应用数据目录。升级后的首次有效额度观察建立新记录基线，使用方法见[使用指南](https://github.com/Dddysun/Codex-Mission-Manager/blob/main/docs/USER_GUIDE.md)。

## Validation and limits / 验证与限制

Automated release gates exercise synthetic quota observations, baseline persistence, transaction rollback, duplicate prevention, attribution boundaries, and native event presentation. Mac runs the full backend and packaging-host suites. Windows coverage remains limited to packaging-host/platform smoke checks and native offline checks. Build, lifecycle, input-layout, and gallery validation remain separate gates. Consult the attached receipts for the result of the selected preview; a skipped or failed check is not a pass.

自动发行检查使用模拟采样覆盖基线持久化、事务回滚、防重复、来源判断边界及原生事件展示。Mac 执行完整后端及打包宿主测试；Windows 保持打包宿主／平台冒烟与原生离线检查范围。构建、退出生命周期、输入框布局和图库仍分别验收，实际结果以所选预览附带的回执为准；跳过或失败不计通过。

Synthetic observations and screenshots do not establish receipt of a real official reset or end-to-end control of a live client. Real quota reset/credit transitions, live pause and recovery, one-time scheduling, keyboard/IME interaction, system notifications, startup, and lock/display-off/sleep/lid behavior remain manual acceptance items. Existing client compatibility limits apply. The Mac preview is ad-hoc signed and unnotarized; the Windows preview is unsigned. This preview does not guarantee closed-lid operation without an external display.

模拟采样与截图不能证明已接收到真实官方重置，或已验收真实客户端端到端控制。真实额度／权益变化、实际暂停续接、一次性预约、真人键盘与输入法、系统通知、登录启动，以及锁屏、熄屏、睡眠和合盖行为仍需人工验收。既有客户端兼容范围继续适用；Mac 为临时签名且未公证，Windows 未签名；本预览不保证无外屏合盖运行。
